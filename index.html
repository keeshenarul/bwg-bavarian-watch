The movements

A German design,
with a Swiss heart.

Movements are essential in every timepiece, so we opt for strong, reliable Swiss calibres. Quartz from the best of Ronda; for automatics we trust the Landeron manufacture Swiss series — the exclusivity of small-batch Swiss engineering with millions-tested reliability.

The motivation
"A dress watch is not necessarily just a dress watch."

When designer Frank Giese set out to develop a new watch in 2018, it was for purely personal reasons. As a watch lover he'd collected everything from luxury houses to flea-market finds — but one watch always carried him through the harder moments.

When he decided the old lady deserved a rest and he deserved a new one, he found… nothing he truly liked. So, with an industrial-design degree and years of experience, he set out to build his own — and a single piece quickly became a company.

Every BWG watch is created on one maxim: style makes life extraordinary in every situation. Maybe a diving bezel is just there to measure when to turn the meat on the grill.

Frank Giese
Founder · Industrial Designer · Munich
